The method

Three acts. Not nine clips.

Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield goes up to 30 seconds a clip, so instead of stitching a pile of 10-second pieces I wrote the whole chase as three acts, each one a timed multishot with every cut written into the prompt. Fewer clips means fewer seams.

Make a character sheet of yourself.Front, side, back and a face close-up, in the outfit you want on screen. Mine is a black bomber, charcoal tee, cargo pants and boots, which reads perfectly in a rainy night chase. This is @Image1 .
Generate Act 1 first.Seedance 2.5, 16:9, 720p, 20 seconds, audio on. Load your character sheet, paste the Act 1 prompt, generate.
Screenshot the car from Act 1.This is the trick. Grab the clearest frame of the supercar and load it as a reference for every clip after this. Without it, the AI swaps the car model between clips.
Generate Act 2 and Act 3.Act 2 uses a cropped close-up of your face, because it opens on an extreme close-up of your eyes. Act 3 uses your sheet plus the car frame.
Stitch, score, and add your CTA.Drop the three acts in order, lay one music track across the whole thing so it feels like one trailer, and put your call to action on the final shot.

What I learned the hard way

Four things that saved this video

Lock the car, not just the face. The AI kept my face perfectly across all three acts, but it swapped the supercar for a different model in Act 2. A screenshot of the car as a second reference fixed it instantly.
Write every cut with a timestamp. "0.0s to 2.5s … 2.5s HARD CUT" gets you an actual edit inside one generation. Twenty seconds, ten shots, one clip.
Use the right reference for the shot. A full-body sheet for wide shots, a face crop when the clip opens on an extreme close-up. The model matches what it can see.
One music track over everything. Each clip's audio is generated separately, so it starts fresh at every cut. One continuous score, with the drop on the explosion, is what makes three clips feel like one trailer.

Don't re-run the whole clip

When one beat is wrong, regenerate only that beat as a short clip and cut it in. It cost me 35 credits instead of 140.
